Where everything came from

What was on the museum site on 1.10.2026, and for each post what was taken from it and what I made up. Back to the review page

1. The museum season page

https://www.tamuseum.org.il/he/series/concert-season-2026-2027/

17 concerts, three strands, prices, dates, players and programmes. One photo for the whole season (Trio Concept). No season identity, no per-concert visual.

2. The ticket pages (16 concerts)

Correction. Earlier I said there were no per-concert photos online. Wrong: the ticket pages carry a press photo for 8 of the 16 concerts, loaded by script, which my first plain fetch could not see. The 31.10 page has a photo of Maya Belsitzman and Matan Ephrat. Pages marked "no photo seen" may just have loaded slowly.

3. Taken from the site's code

The mark, a vector file in the site's stylesheet: logo SVG. Symbol only. The ticket pages also show the full logo with the museum name in Hebrew and English, which I did not use yet.

Colours and font from the site stylesheet, counted by how often they appear:

#22242A ink, 48 uses
#FF2C2C red, 27 uses
#F1F1F1 paper, 16 uses

Font: Greta (Typotheque, paid). I used free Heebo and Frank Ruhl Libre instead.
